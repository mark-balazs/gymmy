In BGF an employer sees aggregates only, never one person's results, and no setting can change that; it sees who is enrolled (a payroll fact), and a withdrawal shows only as 'no longer enrolled'. Whoever sells to companies sees only commercial data, and the account always belongs to the person, not the employer.

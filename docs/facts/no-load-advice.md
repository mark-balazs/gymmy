gymmy never tells anyone what weight to lift or how many reps to do next. There is no suggested weight, no 'add a rep', no 'ready for more weight' card and no warning that a set looked too easy, anywhere in the app.

Rep ranges and set counts stay on plans, because they are the shape of the week, not advice to add weight.

'Home' limits the week to the 53 exercises marked as working at home (mostly dumbbells, kettlebells and bodyweight) and 'Gym' uses the whole library; an emphasis (shoulders, arms, glutes, back or chest) gets a little extra. A fixed per-account offset gives two people with the same answers the same shape but different exercises.

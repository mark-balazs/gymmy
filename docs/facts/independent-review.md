Everything made for gymmy reaches Mark once a fresh agent that did not do it scores it 8 or more out of 10 against its rules; below 8 it is fixed and a new reviewer scores it again. Nothing is kept from him, and each piece, decisions included, ends with its score. Work in progress may be pushed, or written where its playbook writes it, if it says its review is still to come; a plain question or a one-line status skips the review. If only he can settle what holds work below 8, he gets that one question.

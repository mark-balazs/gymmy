Everything made for gymmy reaches Mark once a fresh agent that did not do it scores it 8 or more out of 10 against its rules; below 8 it is fixed and a new reviewer scores it again. Nothing is kept from him, and each piece, decisions included, ends with its score. If only he can settle what holds work below 8, he gets that one question. A pull request may be merged without him once all of it has scored 8 or more and all its checks are green.

Working defaults until Mark answers (GYM-162): a plain question with no options, a one-line status, or a grill's questions skip the review; and work in progress may be pushed, or written where its playbook writes it, if it says its review is still to come.

DOTS uses only barbell back squat, bench press and conventional deadlift, the published per-sex formula and no age factor. It is blank, never smaller, until all three have an estimate in the last eight weeks, there is a bodyweight and sex is male or female ('prefer not to say', the default, leaves it blank), and the screen says which is missing.

In Buttons mode each Train card gets one-tap chips: under the weight, the three or four most recent distinct weights logged on that lift (no row until it has any, and never a weight not yet lifted); under the reps, every number in the exercise's rep range. The −/+ buttons stay for one-step nudges; Ruler and Load the bar are unchanged, and there is no setting to switch the chips off.

The owner's reason: in the gym the reps change every set and weight jumps take many taps, so one tap should set the number.

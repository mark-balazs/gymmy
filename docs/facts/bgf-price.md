BGF pricing is not settled: two shapes stay open, and they can be combined as a small platform fee plus seats. Course seats are €120–180 per participant per 8-week course, two courses a year, which is what a certified course refunds (€75–120) and the only shape Door 2 allows, since a certified course cannot be a subscription. A headcount licence is €10–15 per employee per month, close to the €137 a year the statutory system itself spends per employee it reaches. The earlier €5 per employee per month is too low for an instructor-designed course: a quarter of the course market. Whatever is charged must stay inside the €600 allowance including 19% VAT, and the instructor's share comes out of it.

Decisions, and anything only Mark can do, reach him one at a time from a queue of GYM tickets. The next waits for his answer, and work goes on meanwhile. Each says in short, plain words what to decide, what it is about and why, the options with a recommendation, and what it unblocks; no ticket or decision number stands in for what it is.

Each is asked with the question tool, never as text in a reply, where one was missed: everything in the question, the review report sent as a file just before it, and work that does not wait on the answer started first. The queue's order is a working default until Mark answers (GYM-162): what harms people first; then what blocks the work in hand; then what blocks the most other work; then the oldest.

Decisions reach Mark one at a time, never several in one message. They wait in a queue, GYM's open Decide and Mismatch tickets, and the next comes only once he has answered the one before. Each is asked in short, plain words: what to decide, the options with a recommendation, what the answer unblocks, and its review score at the end.
